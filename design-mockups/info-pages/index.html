    Using this site

    By using pendyora.com you agree to these terms. We may update them from time to time; the date at the top of this page shows when they last changed, and the version on the site when you place an order is the one that applies to it.

    Prices & pricing errors

    We work hard to show accurate prices. If we find a pricing error, we'll correct it as soon as we can. We may cancel an order that contains a pricing error, even after you've received an order confirmation, and we'll refund anything you've paid in full.

    Prices are shown in the currency you choose in the header. Your card is charged in that currency.

    Imports, duties & taxes

    For international orders, goods are imported on behalf of the buyer. You authorise Pendyora, or a customs broker we appoint, to import them for you. Duties and taxes are paid by the buyer in addition to the purchase price unless your order summary shows them as prepaid.

    Custom orders

    Custom-made and customised pieces can't be returned. This includes upgraded stones, modifications to an existing design and new designs made to your brief.

    Layaway

    Our interest-free layaway plan lets you reserve a piece and pay for it over 3 to 24 months. Your price is locked when you pay the deposit, even if our price later goes up. We ship as soon as the balance is paid.

    How layaway works

    1. Choose your pieceLayaway is available on pieces of $200 or more.
    2. Pick a planChoose between 3 and 24 months; the balance is split into equal monthly payments.
    3. We ship when it's paidYour piece is set aside, then shipped insured once the final payment clears.

    Good to know

    • Layaway orders can be cancelled before the final payment; the deposit is refundable less a processing fee.
    • Missed payments may cancel the plan after we've contacted you.

    Changes to these terms

    We may change these terms at any time. Using the site after a change means you accept the updated terms, so please check this page now and then.

        US shipping

        MethodDeliveryCost
        Standard insured3–5 business days after dispatchFree
        Express insured1–2 business days after dispatch$35

        Orders ship in our presentation box, discreetly labelled. You'll get tracking by email as soon as your order leaves us.

        Holiday shipping

        Order by 15 December for standard delivery before 25 December, or by 19 December with express. Made-to-order pieces need longer; their date is shown on the product page.

        US returns

        You can return unworn pieces in their original packaging within 30 days of delivery for a full refund to your original payment method.

        Engraved, resized and custom pieces are final sale.

        International shipping & returns

        We deliver worldwide. Duties and taxes are calculated at checkout and shown before you pay. International returns are accepted within 30 days; return shipping is free, but duties paid on import may not be refundable.

        How to return an order

        1. Start your returnGo to your account, choose the order and select "Return", or contact us.
        2. Pack it securelyUse the original box and include all certificates.
        3. Ship it insuredUse the prepaid, insured label we email you.
        4. Get your refundWe inspect and refund within 5–7 business days of receiving it.
