The piece

The Aurelia is our most requested solitaire. A single round brilliant sits in a six-claw head that rises from a tapered 1.8 mm band. The setting is low, so the ring wears comfortably and lets a wedding band sit flush beside it.

Each ring is cast, set and polished by hand. It leaves the atelier with its grading report and a care card signed by the setter.

The atelier

Fourteen pairs of hands, one ring

An Aurelia takes three weeks to make. It is drawn, cast, set and polished by the same small team, and every stage is signed off before it moves on.

  1. i.DrawingProportions set to your carat and finger size. Days 1–2.
  2. ii.CastingRecycled gold poured, then hand-filed and refined. Days 3–8.
  3. iii.SettingSix claws shaped by hand around your stone. Days 9–14.
  4. iv.FinishingPolished, engraved and inspected under 10×. Days 15–18.
