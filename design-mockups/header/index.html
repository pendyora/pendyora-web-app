Header & mega menu mockup

Hover or focus a menu item to open its panel. Click Search to see the search panel. Below 1024px the menu moves into a drawer behind the menu button. Scroll down to see the header compact.

Opens on intent

A short delay stops panels flashing as the cursor crosses the bar. Moving between items swaps panels without closing.

Keyboard

Tab to an item and press Enter or ↓ to open it. Esc closes the panel and returns focus to its item.

Built from data

Every panel and the mobile drawer render from one menu object, so the CMS or API can drive both.
