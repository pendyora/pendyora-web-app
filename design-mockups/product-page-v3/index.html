The details

Everything about your Aurelia

The Aurelia is our most requested solitaire. A single round brilliant sits in a six-claw head that rises from a tapered 1.8 mm band. The setting is low, so the ring wears comfortably and a wedding band sits flush beside it.

Each ring is cast, set and polished by hand, then signed off by the setter who made it.

Metal
18K Yellow Gold, recycled
Centre stone
1.50 ct natural round brilliant
Band width
1.8 mm tapering to 1.6 mm
Setting height
6.2 mm
Metal weight
≈ 2.9 g (size 6)

Remove before swimming, the gym and the garden. Soak in warm water with a drop of mild soap, then brush gently behind the stone with a soft brush.

Bring it to any boutique once a year for a free clean, claw check and re-polish.

Complimentary insured shipping, signature on delivery. Made-to-order pieces ship in 15–20 business days.

Return within 30 days for a full refund. Resizing is free for the first 60 days.
