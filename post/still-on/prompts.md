## STYLE block (paste at the end of every shot)

```
JEWELRY: Use the exact DazzBliss jewelry from the attached product photos and reference image. Rose gold only. Two layered rose-gold necklaces (one with small diamond drops, one with an interlocking double-circle pavé pendant), small diamond stud earrings, thin rose-gold stacking rings with a flower-shaped diamond cluster ring, and a rose-gold charm bangle with heart and tree charms. Do not change, add, remove or redesign any jewelry. Same woman as the reference image: blonde beachy waves, freckles, white linen shirt over a nude top.

STYLE: Photorealistic, shot on ARRI Alexa Mini, 4K, vertical 9:16, golden hour natural sunlight, soft warm highlights, gentle film grain, natural colour grade, real skin texture with pores and freckles, natural hand anatomy with exactly five fingers, real fine jewelry with accurate rose-gold reflections and natural diamond sparkle, smooth realistic motion.

AVOID: CGI look, plastic or airbrushed skin, distorted hands, extra fingers, melting or morphing jewelry, jewelry changing between frames, oversized fake diamonds, cartoon sparkles, text, logos, watermarks, warped faces, unnatural body motion.
```
